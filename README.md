## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmLink – A Direct Marketplace for Farmers and Consumers

Our proposed solution is a digital platform called FarmLink, which connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, hotels, supermarkets and other bulk buyers.

Currently, agricultural products pass through several intermediaries before reaching consumers. This often reduces the profit received by farmers and increases the final price of products. FarmLink aims to reduce this gap by providing a common platform where farmers can list their products, update quantities and set their expected prices.

Buyers can browse available products, compare prices and place orders directly. The platform will also provide logistics support to help farmers arrange transportation and deliver products to buyers.

An AI-based feature can estimate future demand using previous sales and seasonal trends. This will help farmers understand which products are likely to be in demand and plan their supply accordingly.

The main aim is to make agricultural trading simpler, more transparent and beneficial for both farmers and consumers.

## Technical Approach
Technologies Used

- Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the website interface.
- Backend: Python with Django to manage users, products and orders.
- Database: MySQL to store farmer details, product information, orders and transactions.
- AI/ML: Python-based machine learning for demand prediction using past sales and seasonal data.
- Maps and Logistics: Google Maps API or an equivalent mapping service to identify delivery routes.
- Payment Integration: A secure payment gateway for online transactions.

Implementation Process

1. Farmers and FPOs register and create their profiles.
2. Farmers upload their available products, quantity, location and expected price.
3. Consumers and bulk buyers search for products and place orders.
4. The system connects buyers with suitable farmers based on product availability and location.
5. Logistics support helps arrange delivery.
6. AI analyses previous sales and seasonal trends to estimate future demand.
7. The platform provides order tracking and transaction details.

Basic Working Flow

Farmer Registration → Product Listing → Buyer Search → Order Placement → Farmer Confirmation → Logistics Arrangement → Product Delivery → Payment

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility

FarmLink can be developed using commonly available technologies such as Python, Django, MySQL and web-based mapping services. It does not require expensive hardware, making it suitable for a student-level prototype.

The platform can initially be tested with a small group of farmers and buyers in a particular area before expanding to other regions.

Potential Challenges

- Farmers in rural areas may have limited technical knowledge.
- Internet connectivity may not always be reliable.
- Transportation costs can affect the final product price.
- Demand and market prices may change frequently.
- Maintaining product quality during delivery can be difficult.

Solutions to Overcome Challenges

- Provide a simple interface with Tamil and other regional language support.
- Allow FPOs or local coordinators to help farmers manage their accounts.
- Connect with existing local transport services.
- Use market price information and previous sales data to support better decisions.
- Include product quantity, quality and delivery details in every order.

Viability

The platform can generate revenue through a small service fee on successful orders or optional subscription plans for bulk buyers. Basic registration and product listing can remain free for farmers to encourage participation.

## Impact and Benefits
Social Impact

- Helps farmers reach more customers without depending entirely on middlemen.
- Gives rural farmers better access to digital markets.
- Encourages the use of technology in agriculture.
- Provides consumers with more information about product prices and sources.

Economic Benefits

- Farmers can receive a better share of the selling price.
- Consumers may get agricultural products at more reasonable prices.
- Bulk buyers can purchase directly from farmers and FPOs.
- Reduced unnecessary intermediaries can improve supply chain efficiency.
- Creates opportunities for local delivery and logistics services.

Environmental Benefits

- Better demand prediction can help reduce excess production and food wastage.
- Route planning can reduce unnecessary travel.
- Faster delivery and better coordination can help reduce the time agricultural products remain in storage.

Overall Impact

FarmLink aims to create a more transparent agricultural marketplace where farmers receive fairer opportunities, consumers get better access to fresh produce, and the overall supply chain becomes more efficient.
